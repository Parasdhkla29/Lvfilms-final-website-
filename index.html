LV Films
01 Are your photographers & cinematographers insured?
Yes, we are fully insured with public liability and professional indemnity insurance, ensuring complete peace of mind for every event we shoot.
02 Do you travel outside London?
Absolutely! While we are based in London, we capture love stories and events all across the UK and are available for destination weddings worldwide.
03 How long does it take to receive our final video/photos?
Our standard delivery time is 8-12 weeks, as we meticulously edit each frame to meet our high cinematic standard. Teaser trailers are typically delivered within 2 weeks!
04 Can we request a custom package?
Yes, indeed. Every wedding is unique. We are more than happy to tailor a bespoke package that aligns perfectly with your timeline and specific wishes.
05 What is your booking process?
To secure your date, we require a 25% non-refundable booking deposit and a signed digital agreement. The remaining balance is due 30 days prior to your event.
06 Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible interest-free monthly payment plans to help split the cost leading up to your big day.
